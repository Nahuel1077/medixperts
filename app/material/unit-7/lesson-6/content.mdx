Unless your employer's policies indicate otherwise, adult patients should generally be addressed using “usted” rather than “tú” or “vos”. Using a formal register helps maintain professionalism, demonstrates respect, and supports a neutral interpreter-patient relationship. Keep in mind that some Language Service Providers (LSPs) may have different preferences regarding the level of formality. Whenever this happens, always follow your employer's policies and procedures."
